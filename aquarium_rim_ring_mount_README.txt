How the holder works
1. Slide the outside tube leg through the closed 16.6 mm ring before reconnecting the hose.
2. Attach the ring carrier to the clamp body's outside face with two M3 x 8 mm screws. The body is designed for two M3 heat-set inserts.
3. Place the C-shaped body over the aquarium rim. Its 4.8 mm slot is sized for nominal 4 mm glass.
4. Tighten the M4 thumb screw gently against the broad soft pad so the clip stays on the glass. Do not tighten hard against bare glass.

Hardware: two M3 x 8 mm screws and matching heat-set inserts for the ring carrier, plus one M4 x 12 mm thumb screw and an M4 hex nut for the glass clamp. Use soft pads between the clamp and glass.

Dimensions and fit assumptions
- Current rigid pipe outer diameter is 16 mm in the fittings model; the ring bore is 16.6 mm, giving 0.3 mm nominal radial clearance.
- Glass thickness is assumed to be 4 mm; the clamp slot is 4.8 mm.
- The ring carrier places the tube centerline about 17 mm outside the glass center plane to align with the modeled outer U leg.
- The mount is a separate two-part printed bracket plus M3/M4 fasteners and soft pads. It is not a one-piece print-in-place assembly.

Research notes
- Aluminum-style holders use a ring or slotted clamp to retain the pipe and a separate screw clamp at the tank edge. Chihiros's aluminum holder is listed for two pipe sizes and glass thickness bands; the cited M variant is listed with a 14.4 mm opening and 4–10 mm glass, so it is not copied directly because this model's pipe is 16 mm OD.
- The AQUARIO NEO Holder instructions describe the central fixture clamping over the tank edge with a threaded rod, then the pipe sitting between that fixture and a separate U-shaped outer shell tightened by two side nuts. This design follows that separate clamp-plus-ring-carrier idea, using two M3 fasteners for the ring carrier and an M4 glass clamp screw.
- The 4.8 mm glass slot includes 0.8 mm total assembly clearance for the assumed 4 mm pane. Soft pads spread pressure; tighten only enough to stop movement.

Prototype notes
- Fit, clamp force, strength, and long-term immersion have not been tested.
- PLA is rigid and can crack under repeated flex. Use the screw clamp as an adjustable stationary mount, include soft pads, and tighten gently. Do not use a hard screw tip directly against the glass.
- Verify your tube's actual outside diameter and tank glass thickness before printing. The modeled pipe is 16 mm OD; a different pipe needs a different ring bore.
